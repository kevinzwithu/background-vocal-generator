# Independent music review

Use a separate reviewer after technical validation. Give it this brief, the
user's musical requirements, source melody/chart, and final plan/raw paths.
Do not include the author's rationale, preferred verdict, or prior critique.
The reviewer reads source and arrangement first, then requests/reads diagnostics.
Keep review read-only and return findings in chat; no new persistent report is required.

## Reviewer brief

Review this BV arrangement as a symbolic score. Identify whether it supports the
lead with coherent phrases, clear combined voicings and meaningful development.
Evaluate entrances, exits and silence in context. Respect explicit user requests.
Do not assume a chorus needs every voice or that empty tracks indicate failure.
For an all-rest arrangement, assess whether the silence fulfills the request.

The six upper/lower harmony roles may rest; their default window is one octave
above/below adjusted LeadDouble. Pad roles are independent. Judge wider intervals,
repetition, parallel motion, tensions and density by their local musical effect.

First form your assessment from source and arrangement. Then consult the
validator's localized evidence, including its sample sizes. Explain any musical
concern from the score and its context. Report findings in prose.

Return:
- Scope: symbolic review, or audio review with the actual audio identified.
- Assessment: no material concerns, revise specific passages, or insufficient evidence.
- Findings only where useful: track(s), exact beat/time or bar, observed evidence,
  musical consequence, confidence, and a scoped suggestion. Separate explicit
  requirement violations from preferences. An empty findings list is valid.
- Significant intentional choices worth retaining and any uncertainty.

Keep the review read-only. The author considers each recommendation, resolves
hard errors and reports any unresolved musical disagreements.
